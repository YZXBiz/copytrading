# Brand icons

The service logos in this folder identify the services CopyTrading connects to. Each logo is a
trademark of its owner and is used only to name that service; no endorsement is implied.

- AI provider logos (Anthropic/Claude, OpenAI, Google Gemini, DeepSeek, OpenRouter, Groq, xAI,
  Mistral, Together AI, Fireworks AI, Cerebras, Moonshot AI/Kimi, Ollama, LM Studio) come from
  [Lobe Icons](https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons) (`@lobehub/icons-static-svg` 1.95.1), under
  the MIT License below.
- The Discord and Telegram logos come from [Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org) 16.34.0, under
  CC0 1.0, drawn in each brand's color.

Each SVG was rendered to a 192-pixel PNG for the app.
